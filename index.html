THE CURRICULUM

Five modules, start to finish

Each module unlocks once you pass the quiz for the one before it — work through it at your own pace.

01

Introduction & Clinical Rationale

Why running gait analysis matters for injury prevention and management, the evidence base and its limits, and where gait analysis fits in the broader clinical picture.

02

Running Gait Analysis Methodology

Video setup, what to look at and why, key kinematic markers, and the subjective intake/screening that precedes the analysis.

03

Interpreting Gait & Linking to Pathology

Reading the patterns — overstriding, cadence, vertical oscillation, foot strike, hip drop/crossover — and mapping faults to associated injuries.

04

Injury-Specific Treatment Pathways

Per-injury clinical reasoning: which gait faults to target for which diagnosis, and how to sequence treatment against your assessment findings.

05

Running Re-Training & Return-to-Run Programming

Cueing strategies, drills, load management, and a structured return-to-run progression.
